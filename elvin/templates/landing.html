AI teams

Mix local and cloud models the way you like

Run fully local with Gemma 3 and Qwen Coder, or bring Claude (Opus for code, Haiku for bug hunting) or ChatGPT in as partners or fallbacks. Pick an effort level and see usage, speed and cost per model to decide what works best.

  • Qwen runs on vLLM for NVIDIA & AMD GPUs, and on Apple-Silicon Metal via Ollama.
  • Every model has its own concurrency limit and circuit breaker - no overload, automatic fail-over.
  • Each project has its own memory; nothing is ever shared between projects.
